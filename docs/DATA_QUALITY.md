## Key findings
- `contact_reason` equals `reason_category` in 100.0% of interactions (6 distinct values). It is too coarse to serve as a card-intent label.
- `mentioned_products`: only 0.65% of 548,680 mentioned product IDs exist in `products` (a broken link in the source), so contacts can't be attributed to cards through it.
- `call_transcripts`: 147,292 rows but only 42 distinct `customer_text` values and 1 distinct `detected_intents`. The texts are templates and can't train an intent classifier, so intent labels are team-generated (step 1c).
- Only 9.98% of card numbers pass the Luhn check (sample of 10,000). The PII guard therefore tokenizes any 15–16 digit number.
- Product currencies: USD: 220,501, COP: 107,975, ARS: 71,524. There is no MXN even though Mexico has the most customers, so amounts must not be assumed to be in local currency.
- 40,515 transactions spell the country `Mexico` instead of `México`; they are normalized in silver.
- customers: 9,316 rows (6.21%) have `last_updated` after the dataset end (2026-06-17), i.e. in the future. They are kept (the snapshot is still the latest version) but should not be trusted for recency logic.
- products: 25,113 rows (6.28%) have `last_updated` after the dataset end (2026-06-17), i.e. in the future. They are kept (the snapshot is still the latest version) but should not be trusted for recency logic.
