## Limitations (stated honestly)
- **Call-center contacts can't be attributed to cards.** Of 548,680 product mentions in `call_center_interactions.mentioned_products`, only 3,562 (0.65%) exist in `products`, and none belong to the interaction's customer. The link is broken in the source, so card demand is measured through complaints, products, and transactions instead.
- `contact_reason` has only 6 coarse categories (identical to `reason_category`), and transcripts are 42 templates, so they can't label card intents.
- The synthetic distributions are close to uniform (e.g. card and non-card complaints have similar SLA-breach rates). The data supports cards as a **large, representative** workload, not as an unusually painful one. The workflow choice also rests on it being the best fit for a safe read + single-write (block) automation with human approval.
