# Card policy corpus (SYNTHETIC)

**These policies are synthetic** and belong to **Banco Cordillera, a fictional bank**. They were written for the Factored Hackathon 2026 prototype and are **not** the terms of any real bank. The challenge allows "a clearly labeled synthetic policy service". Every document repeats this notice.

- `es/`: Spanish, the full set.
- `pt/`: Brazilian Portuguese translations of the most-asked policies.

Structure: one policy per file. Each `## ` heading is a section, and a section is the retrieval and citation unit, cited as `doc_id#section-number`.
